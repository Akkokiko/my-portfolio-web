KRAMPUS

Trailr

About the project

Krampus was a game that we started during the 2 days Culture Jam in 2024 and later polished to be presented on digital dragons 2025 and ZTGK. It was supposed to be a simple game in which your task is to eat all naughty children and avoid nuns protecting kids.

My role

While working on the project I was responsible for creating 3D assets, animation as well as programing and designing.

Modeling

Eniviroment

I used Blender and Substance Painter for all modeling. We really wanted to implement the austrian culture in our game so most of propos were based on austrian furnatures.
Later on we decided to add black outlines not only for stylization, but the readability of the game as well.

Krampus

The design of Krampus is the work of our concept artist. My task was to bring her vision in a 3D space. The rig was exported as a non-hierarchy with only a deformation layer, to ensure that animations look good in the engine. Concept art by Lena Stefankowska.

Animations

Krampus

I always thought about Krampus as this barely moving mechanical puppet that doesn't really bother to look or act like a human. The huge part of displaying this tread of hers, was making head and arms follow through. Another important part of her design was the key on Krampus' back. It's always moving as if being the sort of source of the puppet being alive.

Design

I was responsible for a few design elements that made the game better and were later praised during the playtests.

  • Throwable boxes
  • Item system
  • Nuns shooting projectiles
  • Dashing
Many of those elements were extensively discussed by the team and actually sometimes implemented behind their backs :)

UX

We wanted to make the game as satisfying as possible. We have achieved that with the use of many well known technics:

  • Camera Shake
  • UI animations
  • Motion blur
  • Sounds
We used LitMotion library that proven itself to be extremely helpful and easy to use.
